# Indian Artisan Rich 3-Line Product Description Dataset

### Overview
This dataset addresses the critical need of converting informal, terse artisan speech (e.g., *'kolhapuri chappal, pure leather, 1000 rs'*) into rich, customer-ready, 3-line e-commerce descriptions highlighting craft heritage, authenticity, and utility.

### 3-Line Description Standard
- **Line 1 (Craft & History)**: Traditional craft identity, geographical origin, and 2-word/century royal/cultural history.
- **Line 2 (Originality & Materials)**: 100% pure authentic materials (vegetable tanned leather, pure zari, raw silk, virgin brass) and handcrafting techniques.
- **Line 3 (Comfort, Utility & Value)**: Wearability, festive/daily elegance, durability, and fair-trade pricing value.
